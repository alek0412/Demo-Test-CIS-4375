Coach Wang was born and raised in China where she trained and coached in the city of Tianjin. She was a member of the China National Youth Table Tennis Team and later coached in Japan. She has won multiple tournaments during her career.

Recent Achievements

  • 2012 US Open: Over-30 Women's Champion & Women's Singles 2nd Place
  • 2010 US Open: Over-30 Women's Champion & Women's Singles 3rd Place
  • 2008 Badger Open: Champion - Open Singles, Women's Singles & Open Doubles. Defeated a male USA National team coach in Open Finals
  • 2008 Houston Open: Women's Singles & Open Champion
