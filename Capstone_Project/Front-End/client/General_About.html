About Us

Houston's first dedicated badminton facility

Our facility

12 Courts

  • Eight (8) high quality maplewood sports courts
  • Four (4) courts with BWF-approved Nagase Kenko badminton mats on top of wood flooring
  • Anchored sports floor system with 64% shock absorption (badminton standard 55%–75%; good for knees)
  • Matte finish on wood flooring provides a low specular reflectance value to avoid glare
  • BWF-approved Victor net posts
  • 6 feet between courts and 8 feet behind courts

High Ceilings

30 foot clearance

Dark colored walls and ceiling

  • Provides contrast for good shuttlecock visibility

Strip lighting positioned between courts

  • Provides even light distribution and reduces glare

Solar panels

On-site renewable energy

Upstairs Mezzanine

  • Includes a weight room and workout equipment along with a large open area for hosting parties/events

Air Conditioning and Heating

  • Air dispersion system designed to give low velocity air distribution, limiting impact on shuttlecock flight

Media Room

  • Comfortable, relaxing and furnished area for kids and parents

Men and Women Locker/Shower Rooms

  • Shampoo and bodywash provided; towels are not provided

Drinks and Snacks Available

Pro Shop

  • Stringing service, managed by Sportsnet International
