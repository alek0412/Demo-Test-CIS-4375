The Facility

Designed to help you play your best

A premium environment with thoughtful details for comfort, safety, and performance.

Courts and lighting

12 premium courts

  • 8 high-quality maplewood sports courts
  • 4 courts with BWF-approved Nagase Kenko mats
  • Anchored sports floor system with 64% shock absorption

Built for visibility

  • Matte wood finish to reduce glare
  • Dark walls and ceiling for shuttle contrast
  • Strip lighting positioned between courts

Comfort and amenities

Player-ready spaces

  • 30-foot ceiling clearance
  • Upstairs mezzanine with workout area
  • Media room for kids and parents

On-site support

  • Locker and shower rooms (shampoo/bodywash provided)
  • Pro shop and racket stringing service
  • Rental rackets, drinks, and snacks available
