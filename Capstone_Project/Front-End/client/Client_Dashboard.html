Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about visiting Houston Badminton Center. Ask the front desk if you need more detail.

Do I have to be a member to play?

Drop-in guests are always welcome and can get a day pass.

Do I have to reserve a court?

Reservations are recommended during busy hours so you have a court when you arrive. Walk-ins are welcome when space allows—check with the front desk.

Do I need to bring my own racket and shuttlecocks? What else should I bring?

You can bring your own equipment or rent rackets and buy shuttles at the front desk. Wear athletic clothing and clean, non-marking court shoes.

Can I come by myself and find someone to play with?

Yes. Open play and social times are a good way to meet other players. Staff can suggest when the gym tends to be busiest with people looking for games.

When is a good time to come?

Weekday mornings and mid-afternoons are often quieter; evenings and weekends are usually busier. Use our availability page or call ahead if you want a specific experience.
